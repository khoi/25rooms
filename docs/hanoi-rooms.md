## Tour order and review

The opening moves from hot breakfast to cool lake air. Midday widens into craft, growing, and learning before the late rooms return to homes and cultural spaces. The ceramic studio studies vessel profiles; the metal workshop studies a hinged fitting. The two river rooms separate storing irrigation equipment from examining plants. The order is a sequence of visits, not a literal same-day route. Compare the five-room bands as morning preparation, public activity, concentrated work, shared afternoon space, and evening return. Each row has a different combination of setting, silhouette, activity, and object set.

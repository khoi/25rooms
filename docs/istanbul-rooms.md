The first five rooms move from the shore into a home. Daytime then follows specialist trade and creative work across the two sides of the city. The afternoon opens into community activity and a waterfront pause; evening returns through music, cinema, and domestic thresholds. The order is a sequence of visits, not a literal same-day route. Compare the five-room bands as morning preparation, public activity, concentrated work, shared afternoon space, and evening return.

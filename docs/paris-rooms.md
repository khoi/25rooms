# Paris: A city behind the courtyard door

A collection about urban care: keeping a building working, tending stock, repairing objects, practicing art, and making space for another person. Eastern neighbourhoods and canal edges balance recognizable river and cultural settings. The strongest architectural motifs are deep doorways, compact attics, long counters, and layered courtyard windows.

These are 25 contemporary, fictional room proposals. Citations support the stated local connections, not exact interiors, businesses, occupants, or production methods. The sequence follows changing light across separate days. Seasonal choices describe the scene, not the weather on the research date. No opening hours, current service schedules, or access permissions are implied.

Use the existing roughly 12-by-12 isometric footprint, two open front edges, printed outlines, and paper, blue, teal, coral, and sun palette. At collection scale, preserve the dominant shape; at normal zoom, reveal the human activity and five clusters; at close view, expose wear and personal discoveries. Keep landmarks within a narrow exterior strip or window. All artwork stays free of lettering, including packaging, diagrams, covers, and equipment displays. Use ordinary contemporary clothing appropriate to the activity.

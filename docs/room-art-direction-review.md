# Hong Kong and New York: centerpiece and material revision

Reviewed 17 September 2026. This revision follows the construction review and changes the actual fixtures: open frames, curved furniture, folded fabric, visible mechanisms, and material-specific surfaces. The print studio and furniture workshop received complete static-scene redraws. The remaining rooms received targeted replacements and refinements within their existing compositions.

The installed and repository create-room skills now treat the original collection as a minimum craft benchmark. Their new art-direction reference requires a striking composition, a developed centerpiece, controlled light, human relationships, and visible discoveries. Object counts and passing code checks do not establish artistic quality.

## Flagship comparisons

Before and after captures used identical 500-pixel and 850-pixel cards. The original press was rendered at the same close width; the original library, kitchen, and greenhouse supplied additional construction references.

Kwun Tong replaces the opaque drying cabinet with an open nine-level rack, the thin table with a joined printing bench and registration hardware, and the generic rear storage with taller industrial architecture. The screen now carries the print and lifts far enough to reveal the paper below. Ink mixing, washing, cutting, drying, and finished proofs occupy connected work areas. The first dark-wall experiment was rejected because its texture competed with the equipment.

The Navy Yard workshop replaces block chairs with curved crests, shaped seats, turned legs, and seven spindles. Its bench has a tool well, dog holes, joined framing, screw hardware, and stock beneath. An arched factory window, braced lumber racks, and warm floor light give the room a stronger silhouette and material identity. The completed chair and chair under construction form a visible pair.

Both prototypes show clearer centerpiece construction and more distinct material behavior than their previous versions. The original press still provides a stronger example of several people performing separate stages of one workflow. This review does not claim that every revised scene surpasses every original.

- All 75 city rooms draw with finite coordinates in 909 sampled animation frames, including all 50 declared Hong Kong/New York loop boundaries.
- Both copies of create-room pass the skill validator and have identical instructions, references, and interface metadata.
- All 50 revised rooms were reviewed in collection, normal, and close captures. The bamboo platform, theater values, and piano occlusion received additional corrected captures.
- The actual gallery renders the print studio at ratio 3 with advancing animation time and no reported errors. The workshop renders at ratio 3 with reduced motion enabled, time held at zero, and no reported errors.
- Captures remain local in `/tmp/room-art-direction-review`; they are review evidence, not additional production assets.

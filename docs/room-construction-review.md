# Hong Kong and New York construction review

The September 17, 2026 revision develops all 50 existing rooms. It adds architectural depth, fitted furniture, equipment construction, material-specific marks, and objects that connect preparation, activity, and storage. These are original design choices for the fictional interiors described in the [Hong Kong brief](hong-kong-rooms.md) and [New York brief](new-york-rooms.md).

The reference was the rendered original collection and its code in `dist/artwork/drawings.js`. The library demonstrates recessed storage and varied contents. The print shop connects paper handling, machinery, drying, and cleanup. The kitchen develops windows, appliances, and domestic habits. The greenhouse builds layered boundaries and varied growing forms. Their construction principles informed the revised [create-room skill](../.agents/skills/create-room/SKILL.md) and its [visual benchmark](../.agents/skills/create-room/references/original-room-depth.md).

## Validation

All 50 rooms were inspected at collection size, a normal card width of 500 pixels, and a close width of 750 pixels. The still views use time zero. Close views use the midpoint of each room's declared loop. The print workshop and Chinatown kitchen were also compared with the original print shop and kitchen at matching widths.

The review corrected the market's hanging rail depth, a window's height at Wan Chai, the harbour window treatment, rear radiator ordering, and fence-cap attachment at Orchard Beach. Static construction stays outside the central animated actions. The existing animation implementations were compared with the previous source and are unchanged in all 50 rooms.

`node scripts/check-rooms.mjs` checks 125 unique room IDs across five collections, layout and hit targets, finite drawing coordinates for 75 city rooms, 909 sampled animation frames, and 50 declared loop boundaries. Both the project skill and installed skill pass `quick_validate.py`.

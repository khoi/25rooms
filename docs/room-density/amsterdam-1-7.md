The prototype was compared at 800px room width against the baseline cargo-canopy and original library, kitchen, and press. The originals informed recessed storage, visible structural supports, connected work stages, and specific foreground clusters. Evidence: `renders/references.png`, `renders/cargo-canopy-before.png`, `renders/cargo-canopy-normal.png`. The originals still have stronger localized lighting than some of these daytime scenes; this pass does not claim to surpass them.

# Cape Town rooms 1–7 density review

Reviewed against the baseline surf store and the original library, kitchen and press at the same 800px room width. Their source, drawing helpers, painter and layout were inspected before drawing. The new work improves occupied boundaries, working fixture construction and purposeful foreground clusters. It does not claim parity with every original room.

All seven rooms were opened at 240px collection scale, individually at 800px and 1400px, and at four selected animation phases. Final corrections were re-rendered and inspected. The 240px images are in [collection.png](renders/collection.png). The prototype comparison is [references.png](renders/references.png), paired with [surf-store-normal.png](renders/surf-store-normal.png). The other six baseline rooms are in [baseline-six.png](renders/baseline-six.png).

Validation passed: `node scripts/check-rooms.mjs` reports 325 unique rooms across 13 collections, valid layout and hit targets, and 275 city rooms drawn through 3969 sampled animation frames including 250 declared loop boundaries. Native PNG buffers for each of these seven rooms at time zero and its exact loop endpoint also match byte for byte.

Room IDs, footprints, palettes, loopSeconds and stillTime are unchanged. The room-local exported person helper has an optional final size argument with a default of 1; only the kitchen and ceramics makers use it. Existing callers retain their previous geometry. No shared helper, index, brief, manifest or main-checkout file was edited. Assets are ready for parent integration; no commit or push was made from this isolated workspace.

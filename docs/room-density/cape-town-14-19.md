Reviewed on 2026-09-18 in the isolated artwork workspace. All six owned modules changed. IDs, footprints, palette, loop durations, still times, and the original contact geometry remain intact. No shared helpers or main-checkout files were edited.

The prototype comparison is [before with original library/kitchen/press](renders/before-originals.png) beside [boat after](renders/boat-model-normal.png), each drawn at an 800px room width. The change is visible without close zoom: the former loose hull supports now belong to a full alignment bed, the lower window wall is an occupied drafting bay, and the foreground holds separate fitted work stations. The originals informed recessed construction, connected work/storage processes, and objects at several heights. These revisions improve furnished depth substantially; they do not claim to surpass every original in lighting or overall craft.

Every room's 240px collection image, 800px normal image, 1400px close image, and four 800px animation samples were opened with `view_image`. The sports mesh and shelter roof were corrected after that review, rerendered, and checked again at normal size.

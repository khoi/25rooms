All work is isolated under this directory. No shared helpers, main checkout files, metadata, animation durations or still times changed. Existing brief grounding was preserved; additions are fictional household and workshop construction, not new claims about documented local processes.

The prototype was compared at 800px against the baseline and original library, kitchen and press in `renders/reference.png`, after studying their source. The final room has visibly stronger occupied boundaries, a constructed game-table base and separate upkeep and tea clusters. It remains a quiet courtyard rather than claiming the depth of the original library.

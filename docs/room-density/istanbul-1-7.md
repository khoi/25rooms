# Istanbul rooms 1–7 density review

All seven owned room modules were revised in this isolated workspace. IDs, footprints, loop durations, still times and the existing live hand/contact geometry were retained. No shared drawing helpers, registry, brief, or main-checkout files were changed.

The prototype comparison used `renders/shore-tea-before.png`, `renders/shore-tea-normal.png` and `renders/reference.png` at 800px per room. The reference sheet includes library, kitchen and press; their source and rendered construction were studied. The revised tea room has a fuller occupied perimeter, more specific service furniture and stronger foreground relationships. This is a visible improvement over the baseline; it is not a claim that these rooms surpass the originals.

Every room's `renders/<slug>-collection.png` (240px), `-normal.png` (800px), and `-close.png` (1400px) was opened and inspected. Each `-phases.png` sheet was also opened, showing four 800px views: start, contact/hold, return, and endpoint. Individual phase images remain alongside the sheets.

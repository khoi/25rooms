# London rooms 1–7 density review

Reviewed the original library, kitchen and press in source and in `renders/before-originals.png` at 800px per room. The bus prototype comparison made the increased fixture construction, occupied boundaries and foreground maintenance visible at normal size. These rooms are substantially richer than their supplied versions; this review does not claim that they exceed the original collection.

Every room was rendered at 240px, 800px and 1400px room width. Every 800px and 1400px room was opened with `view_image`; the seven 240px renders were opened together at their native width in `renders/collection-all.png`. Four selected animation states per room were opened in the corresponding phase sheet. Final corrected close views were opened again. All paths below are relative to this workspace.

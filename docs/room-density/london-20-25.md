# London rooms 20–25 visual review

All six owned rooms were substantially redrawn in their existing drawing phases. The footprint, lettering-free palette, IDs, loop duration, still time, and moving hand/object coordinates are preserved. No shared helpers or main-checkout files were edited.

The café prototype was compared at 800px against its baseline and the original library, kitchen, and press. Its counter is now a working cabinet and pastry display; its service wall has architectural depth; the preparation and towpath edges now explain storage, cleaning, and waiting. The foreground and rear carry distinct objects while the cup exchange remains open. This is a visible gain in architecture, centerpiece construction, and discoverable relationships. The originals remain the craft reference; this review does not claim that primitive counts establish equivalent quality.

All image paths below are beneath `renders/`. For every slug, `-collection.png` is 240px, `-normal.png` is 800px, and `-close.png` is 1400px room width. Every listed size and motion image was actually opened and inspected. Motion images are 480px room width. Normal/close images were used to judge construction and occlusion; phase images were used to check gesture, clearance, and return.

# Mexico City rooms 8–13 density revision

The six owned room modules were revised in this isolated workspace. IDs, palette, footprints, loop durations, still times, and the principal animation trajectories remain intact. New details are distributed within the original under/live drawing phases. No shared helpers, registry, brief, or main-checkout files were edited.

The metal-window prototype was compared at 800px per room with its baseline and the original library, kitchen, and press. `renders/before-originals.png` and `renders/prototype-after-original.png` were opened. The principal gains are fitted wall occupation, constructed secondary work areas, and purposeful foreground objects; these are substantial improvements over the six baselines, without a claim that every original's craft level has been exceeded.

Every room's individual `-collection.png` (240px), `-normal.png` (800px), and `-close.png` (1400px) was rendered and opened. Individual `-start.png`, `-return.png`, and `-endpoint.png` images were also opened. The normal/close still is the contact or hold pose except for community-pool, whose additional `-hold.png` at 8s was opened. Paths below are relative to this workspace.

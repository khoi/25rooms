# Somewhere else density revision

All five final rooms were opened individually at 240px, 800px and 1400px using view_image. Their 800px phase renders at 0, 3, 7 and 11 seconds were inspected. The still render is the 0-second state. No animation timing or contact geometry changed. Images live in `renders/`: `<room-id>-240.png`, `<room-id>-800.png`, `<room-id>-1400.png` and `<room-id>-t0.png`, `-t3.png`, `-t7.png`, `-t11.png`.

Prototype comparison: `renders/before-originals.png` contains the baseline caravanserai and original library, kitchen and press at 800px each. `renders/desert-prototype.png` is the revised prototype. Their source construction was studied as well as their rendered form. The final changes strengthen architectural depth and fixture construction; they do not establish that these rooms surpass the originals in lighting or animation complexity.

# Paris rooms 14–19 density revision

All six owned modules were revised in this isolated workspace. No shared helpers, indexes, briefs, or main-checkout files were changed. IDs, footprints, palette, still times, loop lengths, and hand contact paths remain intact. The seed drawer runners now travel with its existing drawer motion.

The source and matching 800px renders of the original library, kitchen, and press were studied. Their recessed storage, useful architectural edges, connected work stages, and supported fittings informed the changes. `renders/references.png` records those references beside the original science room. The prototype comparison is `renders/science-table-before.png`, `renders/science-table-normal.png`, and `renders/original-press-normal.png`.

The prototype visibly gains occupied architectural edges, a built focal support, distinct preparation and drawing areas, and foreground equipment. Its lens and human contact remain the strongest central silhouette. The originals retain more nuanced atmospheric lighting in places; this revision does not claim to exceed them.

Every file named below was opened with `view_image`, including the normal and close views individually. For each slug, the evidence prefix is `renders/<slug>-`; `collection.png` is 240px, `normal.png` is 800px, and `close.png` is 1400px. `start.png`, `return.png`, and `endpoint.png` are 800px. The normal still is the contact/hold phase except for the bridge, whose additional `hold.png` is 8.2 seconds.

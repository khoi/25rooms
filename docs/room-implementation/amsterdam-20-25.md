# Amsterdam rooms 20–25 implementation review

Implemented the six assigned room modules with exact brief IDs, a 12 × 12 footprint, the existing ink palette, and explicit loop and still times. Shared helpers and collection integration remain untouched. No generated raster assets are used.

Reviewed real `RoomPainter` output through `/tmp/rooms-200-render/render.mjs`. The first 800 px comparison was against the original kitchen and loom, after studying the library, kitchen, and loom source. The new rooms have more explicit large fixtures than the kitchen reference, but their lighting remains flatter than the original kitchen. No claim of surpassing the originals is made.

All six rooms were viewed at 220 px collection scale, 800 px normal scale, and 1400 px close scale.

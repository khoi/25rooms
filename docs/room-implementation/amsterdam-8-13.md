# Amsterdam rooms 8–13 implementation review

The six modules use the existing canvas renderer, 12 by 12 footprint, and shared ink palette. Each module declares its loop duration and still time. Figure clip names begin with `amsterdam-` and remain local to their scene.

All six rooms passed a direct-import coordinate check over 85 times per room, including 0, every eightieth of the cycle, and both sides of the loop boundary. The checker also executed queued drawing pieces. The native render harness executed the real `RoomPainter` for every image below.

Every room was viewed at 220 pixels, 800 pixels, and 1400 pixels wide. Eight rendered phases per room were inspected, including the contact, reveal, return, settling pose, and loop boundary. The final collection and normal sheets are `/tmp/amsterdam-8-13/collection.png` and `/tmp/amsterdam-8-13/normal.png`.

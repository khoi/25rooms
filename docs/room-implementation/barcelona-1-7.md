# Barcelona rooms 1–7 implementation review

Implemented from the existing Barcelona brief, 18 September 2026. The seven modules retain the 12 by 12 footprint and existing palette. Each has an explicit loop duration and intentional still time. No shared helpers, registry, renderer, existing rooms, or brief were edited.

All seven were rendered with the shared native canvas harness using the real `RoomPainter`. I viewed the 220 px collection sheet, every 800 px individual still, every 1400 px close view, held action frames, the closing poses, and representative transition sheets. Evidence is outside the repository in `/tmp/barcelona-1-7/`. The direct-module validator (`node /tmp/barcelona-1-7/check.mjs`) performed 77 successful finite-coordinate drawing passes: seven static layers and ten animation phases per room, including each individual loop boundary. Pixel comparisons of the scene area immediately before and after the loops differ by 0.0000% of pixels above an eight-channel-value tolerance for six rooms and 0.0002% for the linen room. That small linen difference follows its continuously moving sheet.

The originals studied in source and rendered at matching width were library, kitchen, and press. Comparison images are `/tmp/barcelona-1-7/comparison-before.png` and `/tmp/barcelona-1-7/comparison-final.png`. The new scenes have distinct silhouettes and clearer individual mechanisms after revision; their broader pale floor areas remain quieter than the original library and press. This review does not claim that these rooms exceed those originals.

For the screenshot column below, filenames are relative to `/tmp/barcelona-1-7/`. Every room also appears in `collection.png`. `*-held.png` records the fully revealed action, while `*-loop-before.png` and `*-loop-after.png` record both sides of its boundary.

# Barcelona rooms 20–25 implementation review

Reviewed on 18 September 2026. These six modules preserve the 12-by-12 footprint, printed palette, and exact brief IDs. Each exports a complete room with `loopSeconds` and `stillTime`.

The native render harness runs the real `RoomPainter`. The review includes a 220-pixel collection view, individual 800-pixel views, individual 1400-pixel views, and seven phase samples per room. The phase samples cover time zero, the three main transitions, the settling pose, and both sides of the loop boundary. Additional intermediate frames checked the landing door and shopping handle. A direct Node check evaluated ten times per room and rejected nonfinite drawing coordinates. Drawing calls match exactly at time zero and at each complete loop duration.

The original greenhouse, library, kitchen, and press supplied construction references. The first rendered comparison places flowers beside the greenhouse and cinema beside the library at 800 pixels: `/tmp/barcelona-20-25/prototype.png`. The flower prototype needed a stronger bucket crown and a foreground pickup relationship. Both were added before the final review. The originals still have more incidental wear in some small surfaces. This review does not claim that every new room exceeds them.

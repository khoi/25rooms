# Cape Town rooms 1–7 implementation review

These seven original room modules retain the 12 by 12 footprint and the existing printed canvas palette. The compositions translate the brief's rear edge onto renderer edges i=0 and j=0. No shared artwork helpers, registry, navigation, or hosting files were changed. The local `person` export in `surf-store.js` draws only a physical figure; each scene owns its furnishings and choreography. Figures are queued as depth-sorted live pieces to keep hands and faces readable in front of handled objects.

Native `RoomPainter` renders were inspected at 220 px collection width, 800 px normal width, and individual 1400 px close width. Every room was also reviewed at zero, the 20/40/60 percent action beats, the start of its closing pause, immediately before the loop boundary, and the exact boundary. The direct checked-hand script `/tmp/cape-town-1-7/check.mjs` executed all seven static layers and 70 live frames, validating finite coordinates and nonempty operations. No custom FIGURES clip names are registered.

The initial comparison `/tmp/cape-town-1-7/comparison.png` places surf-store and rope-drying beside original press and kitchen at equal 800 px widths. `/tmp/cape-town-1-7/library-comparison.png` compares the ceramics room with the original library. The originals retain greater wall density and more varied figure styling. These rooms use clearer isolated silhouettes and more open circulation; the comparison does not establish that they exceed the originals.

All evidence paths below are local temporary files, outside the repository. Shared collection evidence is `/tmp/cape-town-1-7/collection.png`; normal evidence is `/tmp/cape-town-1-7/final-normal.png`. Each `*-final-close.png` was opened after the depth-order fix. The final kitchen cover position and pocket flattening were also checked in `/tmp/cape-town-1-7/revised-final-phases.png`.

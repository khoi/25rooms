The direct validation script is `/tmp/cape-town-20-25/check.mjs`. It verifies IDs, the 12-by-12 footprint, duration and still metadata, finite drawing coordinates, and 96 sampled live frames. Samples include zero, each fifth of the loop, both sides of the boundary, and the still. All six modules pass. The rooms do not register shared figure clips or change shared helpers.

The marimba is the strongest centerpiece in this range. Its bars, suspended cord, stepped resonators, and braced frame remain readable at normal size. The veranda has the clearest architectural silhouette. The comparison with `original/loom` identified useful construction and figure-placement improvements. It does not establish that these rooms exceed every original-room benchmark.

The source study covered the original library, loom, and kitchen implementations. The review inspected every assigned room at collection, normal, and close scale, then inspected the primary phase sheets and revised contact frames. The native renderer does not test application navigation or reduced-motion selection. Those checks remain part of root integration.

All six rooms were inspected at 220 px, 800 px, and 1400 px per room. Collection and normal views are in `/tmp/hanoi-20-25/final-collection.png` and `/tmp/hanoi-20-25/final-normal.png`. The original kitchen and press were reviewed beside the first implementations in `/tmp/hanoi-20-25/first-comparison.png`. The café has the strongest architectural silhouette. The supper table has the clearest object reveal. These observations do not claim that the rooms exceed the originals.

`/tmp/check-hanoi-tail.mjs` checked direct imports, static drawing, finite coordinates, and live drawing at every beat boundary, each still time, and both sides of every loop boundary. `/tmp/rooms-200-render/hanoi-tail-loop.mjs` compared rendered pixels at 0 s and each room's declared duration. All six pairs were identical. The comparison across the boundary at ±0.001 s changed between 3 and 158 pixels per room, consistent with the small continuous secondary motion.

The motion sheets show six phases per room, including the initial pose and the final millisecond. Final application navigation, reduced-motion integration, and the city registry remain with the coordinating agent. No shared helpers or application files were changed. The `technical-writing` skill guided this record. Its referenced `unslop` skill was absent from both installed skill directories.

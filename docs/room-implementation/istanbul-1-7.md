# Istanbul rooms 1–7 implementation review

Review date: 18 September 2026. The seven modules use the existing canvas palette and 12-by-12 footprint. Each exports `loopSeconds` and `stillTime`. Shared helpers remain unchanged.

The prototype comparison is `/tmp/istanbul-1-7/prototype.png`. It places shore tea and the bakery beside the original kitchen and press at 800 pixels per room. The tea room has developed boundaries and clear service equipment, with a calmer foreground than those originals. This review does not claim that the new rooms exceed the originals.

The native harness executes `RoomPainter`. All seven rooms were inspected at 220 pixels in `/tmp/istanbul-1-7/collection.png`, at normal size, and in individual 1400-pixel still renders. Six sampled animation frames per room cover the opening, working motion, pause, return, and final pose. Direct module checks passed 70 frames, including both sides of every loop boundary, with finite drawing coordinates. City-prefixed figure clip names remain unique. Root owns integrated browser verification.

# London rooms 8–13 implementation review

The six modules preserve the 12 by 12 footprint, five-ink palette, and canvas drawing system. Each module owns its composition and figure clips. Shared geometry files remain unchanged.

Source study covered the original `library`, `press`, and `greenhouse` rooms. The first visual comparison used `bicycle-wheel` beside `press` at 800 pixels. The wheel room has a clearer circular center and a deep arch, while the original press has denser small equipment and stronger local lighting. This review does not claim that the new rooms exceed the originals.

The native `RoomPainter` harness rendered every room at 220, 800, and 1400 pixels. Every still image and phase sheet listed below was inspected. The phase sheets include the start, contact, transition, hold, return, final pause, and both loop endpoints. A direct-import check passed for six static layers and 60 live frames with finite drawing coordinates. Exact live drawing traces also agree at time zero and the declared loop duration for all six rooms. Results are saved in `/tmp/london-8-13/check-results.json`.

The strongest room is `shared-stair`: its diagonal flight, folded buggy, and two neighbours remain distinct at all three sizes. The maritime model has the most developed central mechanism. No import, finite-coordinate, or loop-boundary failures remain in the assigned modules. Integrated navigation and reduced-motion behavior remain with the coordinating agent.

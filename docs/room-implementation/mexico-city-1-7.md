# Mexico City rooms 1–7 implementation review

Seven modules were implemented from the existing brief. Each exports a 12-by-12 world with the exact brief ID, the prescribed loop duration, and an intentional `stillTime`. Drawing is local to each module; shared geometric helpers were read but not changed. No figure clip names were added.

The source study covered the greenhouse, kitchen and press originals. Their matching-width comparison is `/tmp/mexico-city-1-7/prototype-comparison.png`. The first chinampa pass had an underused shelter wall and an excessive reach. The reviewed revision adds a supported drying/twine shelf, closer grips and a grower beside the tray. The new rooms have distinct envelope and main-fixture silhouettes. They do not match the originals' depth of localized lighting and incidental household detail; no claim of exceeding the originals is made.

All seven were rendered with the shared native RoomPainter harness at 220, 800 and 1400 pixels. The collection image is `/tmp/mexico-city-1-7/collection.png`; the final normal sheet is `/tmp/mexico-city-1-7/final-normal.png`. Individual normal and close views were opened, as were all six-frame phase sheets. These use time zero, the first three principal transition boundaries, the settling frame, and the last frame before the loop. Numerical checks also executed both sides of each loop boundary, the declared still time, and intermediate frames with coordinate validation. Root owns the integrated browser review.

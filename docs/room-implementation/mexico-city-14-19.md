# Mexico City rooms 14–19

Implemented against `docs/mexico-city-rooms.md` using the existing canvas renderer and palette. The six modules retain the 12 × 12 footprint and expose their brief duration and an intentional `stillTime`. No shared helpers, registry, collection index, application files, or publication settings were changed.

The source study covered the original library's recessed storage, greenhouse's structural planting tiers, and kitchen's supported work surfaces and localized light. Rendered comparisons are `/tmp/mexico-city-14-19/boat-comparison.png` and `/tmp/mexico-city-14-19/original-references.png`. These new scenes have lighter boundaries and more open work pockets than the dark original library and kitchen; this review does not claim that they surpass those originals.

Native `RoomPainter` renders were inspected at 220 pixels per room, individual 800-pixel normal views, and individual 1400-pixel close views. The collection image is `/tmp/mexico-city-14-19/collection.png`. Still images below use each module's declared still pose. Motion sheets at 0, 4.8, 9.6, 14.4, 22, and 24 seconds are `/tmp/mexico-city-14-19/motion-<time>.png`; the mask and repair revisions also have individual start and hold evidence. Native rendering exercises the real static/live layers, drawing operations, and figure clips, but integrated browser navigation remains the coordinating agent's check.

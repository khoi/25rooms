The first park prototype was compared with the original press at 800px in `park-comparison.png`. The comparison led to the storage and drying-rack additions. `originals-comparison.png` places the original library, kitchen, and bridge beside the roof, music, and patio rooms at the same width. The originals retain denser inventories and stronger night-light contrast. The new rooms use distinct silhouettes, clear work areas, and exposed construction; this review does not claim that they surpass the originals.

Validation used direct module imports because collection integration belongs to the coordinating agent. `/tmp/mexico-city-20-25/check.mjs` checked finite drawing coordinates in six static layers and 301 animation states, including all loop boundaries. `/tmp/mexico-city-20-25/boundaries.mjs` rendered native pixels at 800px.

# Paris rooms 14–19 implementation review

Reviewed on 2026-09-18. Each module preserves the 12 by 12 footprint and the five-ink palette. The figures use local articulated drawing functions, so these modules add no shared figure clips.

The first science-room render was compared with `original/library` at 1000 pixels. The review added a tactile foreground bench, enlarged the visitors, and brought the educator closer to the tray. `original/kitchen` and `original/press` were also inspected at 800 pixels. The new rooms have lighter boundaries and simpler figures than those references. This review does not claim that they exceed the originals.

A direct drawing check sampled ten states per module, including both sides of the loop boundary. All 60 states passed finite-coordinate checks.
